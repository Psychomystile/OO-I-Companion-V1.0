VERSION 0.23 — MAXIMUM HEAT ACTION ROLLS
At maximum Heat, named actions and the manual Actions + counter open a D12
Heat Threshold check. This includes actions that reach the maximum and Cold
shots made while already at maximum Heat. Available Actions still limit use.
Enter or roll the D12, resolve any repeats / impact / weapon dice, then confirm.
Effects apply to that Rig without Exhaust, Ruptured damage or round advancement.
Cancel action restores the pre-action trackers and recorded action list.
Both normal games and campaign Engagements use this behavior.
All previous features and images are included in this complete package.

VERSION 0.22 — ACTION HISTORY
Named actions appear below Actions used, with weapon details and Heat cost.
Undo and decreasing Actions remove the corresponding recorded entries.
History is saved with battle trackers and clears at the next round.
Manual counter changes do not invent an action name. Complete package.

VERSION 0.21.1 — PIVOT BUTTON REMOVED
Move includes one Pivot up to 90 degrees at its start or end.
No separate Pivot button or additional Action/Heat charge.
All earlier features are included.

VERSION 0.21 — SELECTABLE CARDS, NO FLOATING DOCK
Click a Rig card or its name in the top bar to select it. Selection does not
end its activation; use Activated / Not activated for that game marker.
The floating bottom dock is removed. Named actions and Undo are inside each
Rig card, alongside the existing Heat, Actions and activation controls.
All previous features, including v0.20 dashboard status icons, are included.

VERSION 0.20 — SQUARE DASHBOARD STATUS BUTTONS
Monochrome vector symbols on dark square buttons, with the status name below.
Active symbols glow ochre. Off / Active labels removed; pressed states remain
available to assistive technology. Permanent conditions stay protected.
Includes all previous changes. Upload the full images folder, including SVGs.

VERSION 0.19 — CONDITION ICON BUTTONS
Condition buttons now sit immediately below the Rig illustration.
Original Stalled, Shocked, Slowed, Immobilised and Ruptured icons are extracted
from Rulebook V1 p.35. Active conditions are highlighted and labelled.
Permanent component conditions remain active; repair the component to remove
their source. Ruptured opens the affected-component checkboxes.
Includes all previous changes. Upload images/status with all other files.
