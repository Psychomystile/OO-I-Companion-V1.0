OF OIL & IRON COMPANION — v0.28.35
ENGAGEMENT REFERENCES

In Game setup, Rulebook p. 95–100 is displayed beside Random Engagement.
The link follows the selected Engagement, including manual and random choices,
in both Quick game and campaign. Clicking it opens that mission’s actual rules
in the existing read-only viewer, including the correct half of shared pages.
Changing the Engagement never opens the rulebook automatically. The link is
also available when campaign setup is locked during an ongoing Engagement.

COMPACT READ-ONLY RULEBOOK

The complete 121-page rulebook remains readable inside the app. The circled
page-picker strip and the highlighted-passage text bar have been removed.
Page references zoom and position the requested content, with no yellow box.
Weapons include their numeric profiles, Equipment includes its effect and cost,
and topic headings lead to their explanatory text instead of the title alone.
The manual download button and its data loader have been removed.

Contents / Search opens a floating list without resizing the page. Click or
tap outside the list to dismiss the list only, including outside the reader.
The dismissing tap does not activate controls underneath. Selecting a chapter
or search result also closes the list and brings its passage into view.

The top-right X exits full-screen reading and restores the floating window,
page, zoom and scroll. In the floating window the X closes the rulebook.
Double-click/double-tap and the Full screen button still change reading mode.
Escape dismisses the current layer: full screen, open list, then the reader.

Pinch with two fingers zooms only the page from 50–400%. Pan the enlarged page
with one finger. At 100% fit width, swipe horizontally to turn pages. On a
keyboard use the left/right arrow keys. Contents and whole-book search remain
available, as do Back and the page number in the header.

Existing manual references open only on click or tap and retain the correct
printed numbering. The old PDF entry point now opens this same read-only
viewer. The 121 page images, full text, original Rig images, game logic,
generator and save format are unchanged. Rig-sheet export is retained.
