OF OIL AND IRON COMPANION - VERSION 0.7.1

UPDATE ON YOUR PC
Extract index.html, app.js and style.css into the SAME project folder,
replacing all three files. Keep their exact names. Open index.html in your
usual browser and press Ctrl+F5. Existing squads and weapon selections are
preserved when you keep the same browser and folder. No extra installation
is needed. All interface text is in English.

MAIN MENU
The app opens with Campaign and Normal game as separate sections.
Campaign > New campaign opens the starting squadron builder.
Existing campaigns can resume setup, the hangar, a battle, or Aftermath.
Normal game > Build squadron opens its independent builder.
Main menu returns to the mode choice without resetting any progress.
Saved squadron templates are filtered by mode.

FACTION FILTER
Recruitment and recovery show only the campaign faction, including wreck
recovery. Old out-of-faction market selections are reset automatically.
Mercenary support remains a separate source showing eligible Nomad Rigs.

MULTIPLE CAMPAIGNS AND NORMAL GAMES
Use Main menu to enter Campaign or Normal game and resume a session.
New campaign creates a separate roster without replacing the current one.
Normal game opens or resumes your independent standard game, even during
a campaign Engagement or an unfinished Aftermath. Each campaign retains
its roster, damage, XP, inventory, history, report and undo actions.
Name campaigns using Campaign name in the hangar or the builder name field.
Existing version 0.5 data becomes the current session automatically.
Imported campaign backups are added as separate campaigns. Export backup
still exports only the selected campaign: export each campaign separately.

SIMPLIFIED HANGAR
Ironclad name is directly editable on each Rig card and saves as you type.
You can also name the Ironclad when creating the squadron.
Pilot identity, XP and level follow the Ironclad when reassigned.
Each card shows deployment, pilot progression and four component statuses.
Open Weapons & Equipment, Repairs, or Rig & pilot management as needed.
Backup and undo controls are under Backup & undo at the top.

COMPLETE WEAPON CATALOG
16 common profiles from Rulebook p. 48, filtered by Rig weight class:
Light: Light Melee Weapon, Auto Gun, Light Cannon, Rivet Gun.
Medium: Medium Melee Weapon, Machine Gun, Heavy Cannon, Rivet Rifle.
Heavy: Heavy Melee Weapon, Heavy Machine Gun, Siege Bombard, Precision Cannon.
Colossal: Demolisher Mortar, Artillery Cannon, Siege Missile Battery,
Siege Howitzer.

These supplement the 77 chassis-specific profiles printed for 24 Rigs.
Common and Faction/Nomad weapons have separate selector groups and source
labels on battle sheets. Each chassis gets the four common profiles for its
own weight class. Profiles sharing a name are not merged or substituted.
Common melee weapons are treated as melee and do not require reloading.
Hull-only slot restrictions and the once-per-Hull-weapon rule are checked.
Faction Rigs and Nomad Rigs have separate catalog and campaign roster sections.

START A CAMPAIGN
1. Choose Roster type > Campaign.
2. Select one main faction, a Skirmish formation, and the starting Rigs.
3. Equip at least two weapons per Rig; spend no more than 15 Iron in total.
   Starting pilots must be Rookie or Experienced. Rig capacities still apply.
4. Choose Review and start campaign, then Start campaign with this roster.
5. In Campaign > Hangar, select the formation and Rigs to deploy.
   Begin next Engagement opens their battle sheets. Other Rigs stay in reserve.

A campaign roster may grow beyond a battle formation. After starting the
campaign, the initial 15-Iron cap and starting-pilot restriction no longer
apply to the evolving roster. Deployment still checks the agreed formation
and each Rig's loadout. Short-handed deployment is available after losses.

BATTLE SHEETS
Component trackers use a compact 2-by-2 layout; weapon profiles are open.
Track Heat, Actions, reload status, temporary Conditions and preparations
manually while resolving the game at the table. Next round resets Actions
used and activation markers only. Resolve maintenance before advancing.
Campaign re-rolls have a per-Engagement usage tracker.

Component damage, Disabled weapons and unresolved Ruptured flags carry into
the next Engagement. Temporary activation state resets. Permanent damage
traits remain attached to the Rig or affected weapon. Consumed Equipment
marked Used / discarded is removed when Aftermath is applied.

GUIDED AFTERMATH
Finish Engagement / Aftermath opens a report that includes:
- Victory, draw or defeat; objective Salvage and enemy destruction credits.
- All 12 battlefield types and their aftermath rewards or environmental damage.
- Separate D12 rolls for Rig recovery and Ironclad survival.
- Individual kills, objectives and additional mission XP.
- Battlefield salvage searches and Equipment table results.
- Underdog rewards and reasoned scenario adjustments.
- A complete preview before applying changes once.

Enter your physical dice results or use the D3, D6 and D12 buttons.
A draw uses Victory rewards, but awards 1 result Renown rather than 3.
Normal objective Salvage/VP and Salvage objectives are entered separately.
Per-Ironclad objective counts award XP and Renown; do not double-count VP.

Rig recovery: 2+ if it did not explode; 7+ if it exploded.
Ironclad survival: 2+ if it did not explode; 5+ if it exploded.
Contracted Recovery Crew adds +2 to the Rig recovery roll only when applicable.
Surviving pilots whose Rigs are lost enter the unassigned Ironclad pool.
A retained Rig whose pilot is lost receives a fresh Rookie.

XP, LEVELS AND RENOWN
XP thresholds: 0, 3, 6, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40.
Levels 4, 7 and 10 grant a mandatory promotion if possible; Elite pilots get
one re-roll instead. Other level perks grant one re-roll per Engagement.
XP and level perks follow an Ironclad when reassigned to another Rig.
Promotions can affect the loadout's Iron cost; adjust the loadout if needed.
The campaign records Renown, Engagements and enemy Rigs destroyed for the
end-of-season tie-break. Season length defaults to 6 and can be changed.
Compare final standings with the other players in your group.

HANGAR, REPAIRS AND IRON MARKET
Damage is not automatically healed. Each Rig gets a 3-SP free repair pool
per Aftermath, shared across its components. Further regular repairs cost
1 Salvage per 3 SP repaired, rounded up.
Disabled -> Catastrophically Damaged costs 1 Salvage.
Catastrophically Damaged -> full Structure costs 1 Salvage.
Each restoration operation adds a unique D6 permanent trait; duplicate rolls
must be rerolled. Iron Market Parts Cache can ignore one new repair trait
per Aftermath. Second-hand traits cannot be removed.

Permanent Iron Capacity, maximum Structure Points, SPD and Exhaust changes
are included automatically. A Heat Exchanger trait opens the preceding Heat
space at modifier +0 while retaining the other printed modifiers. Armour,
weapon accuracy/Strength, backfire and Heat-result modifiers are reminders
for manual resolution at the table. Common and faction profiles retain their
printed base combat values.

Buy new or second-hand chassis, equip weapons and Equipment, assign pilots,
claim battlefield rewards, store gear, sell stored items, or scrap chassis.
New chassis cost 4/8/12/16 Salvage and receive 5/5/6/6 Iron for weapons.
Extra weapons and Equipment cost their printed Iron cost in Salvage.
Second-hand chassis cost half and require four trait rolls plus four D3
component damage rolls. The initial Arms trait applies to the left weapon.
Stored weapons retain weapon-specific traits. Compatible stored weapons are
matched to their original chassis profile, preserving different printed
profiles for similarly named weapons.

The Experimental Load-Bearing Frame can be assigned to another owned Rig.
A lost target must be reassigned before the affected Rig can deploy.
Mercenary hiring is once per campaign, for one Engagement. Mercenaries use
a separate support slot and their contractor allowance, cannot secure
objectives or earn XP, and leave after the Engagement. Their gear cannot be
transferred to the player's inventory. Agree their pilot grade at the table.

REVIEWABLE GROUP RULINGS AND MANUAL ALLOCATIONS
The manual leaves some details open; the app shows the choice instead of
silently treating an interpretation as an additional printed rule:
- Environmental damage: choose affected components at the table. If there
  are fewer eligible Rigs, only the available targets are required.
- Free repair credits: the checkbox treats one credit as one selected repair
  operation; use that only with group agreement, or record an adjustment.
- Battlefield Recovery Rig: add the agreed D3 number of extra search rolls
  separately from the normal per-Rig search.
- Settlement: choose whether reserve/unassigned pilots also receive its XP.
- Underdog: choose the applicable D3 lower-value or D6 short-handed award.
  Compare the post-reward roster values with the opponent; scenario
  adjustments can record a different interpretation or mission rule.
- Free battlefield chassis are treated as new recruits with a normal weapon
  allowance. Adjust if your group uses a different interpretation.
- Salvage result 13: only a Rig destroyed in that Engagement is eligible.
  Enter its actual component state. No weapon allowance is granted. Use the
  recorded adjustment tools to reconstruct inherited gear and traits from
  its original sheet. If no eligible Rig exists, remove the unused wreck
  claim with a reasoned adjustment.

The app does not simulate all combat, arbitrate mission objectives or keep
an opponent's hidden roster. Those facts are supplied in the report.
Scenario adjustments, inherited wreck details and agreed rulings are logged.
The report handles reward accounting and progression together; you make
repair purchases in the hangar after reviewing/applying the report. Keep
any order-sensitive scenario exceptions aligned with the group's rules.

SAVES, BACKUPS AND CORRECTIONS
The current campaign saves automatically in the browser. The last five
campaign transactions can be undone, including an applied Aftermath.
Engagement history retains the reports, entered rolls and casualty outcomes.
The transaction ledger records market, repair and manual changes.
Export a JSON backup after each Engagement. Import restores a campaign on
another browser/device or after a browser reset. Export first before using
New campaign. Browser saves do not sync between devices. Clearing browser
data deletes local saves; private browsing or storage limits may prevent
saving. The app displays a warning if saving fails.
Keep the three application files in the same folder to retain existing local
saves. Old campaign-start rosters can be initialized as campaigns; existing
standard squads remain standard. Previously saved weapon indices are retained.
