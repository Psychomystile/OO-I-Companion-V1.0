GUIDED AFTERMATH
Finish Engagement / Aftermath opens a report that includes:
- Victory, draw or defeat; objective Salvage and enemy destruction credits.
- All 12 battlefield types and their aftermath rewards or environmental damage.
- Separate D12 rolls for Rig recovery and Ironclad survival.
- Individual kills, objectives and additional mission XP.
- Battlefield salvage searches and Equipment table results.
- Underdog rewards and reasoned scenario adjustments.
- A complete preview before applying changes once.

Enter your physical dice results or use the D3, D6 and D12 buttons.
A draw uses Victory rewards, but awards 1 result Renown rather than 3.
Normal objective Salvage/VP and Salvage objectives are entered separately.
Per-Ironclad objective counts award XP and Renown; do not double-count VP.

Rig recovery: 2+ if it did not explode; 7+ if it exploded.
Ironclad survival: 2+ if it did not explode; 5+ if it exploded.
Contracted Recovery Crew adds +2 to the Rig recovery roll only when applicable.
Surviving pilots whose Rigs are lost enter the unassigned Ironclad pool.

HANGAR, REPAIRS AND IRON MARKET
Damage is not automatically healed. Each Rig gets a 3-SP free repair pool
per Aftermath, shared across its components. Further regular repairs cost
1 Salvage per 3 SP repaired, rounded up.
Disabled -> Catastrophically Damaged costs 1 Salvage.
Catastrophically Damaged -> full Structure costs 1 Salvage.
Each restoration operation adds a unique D6 permanent trait; duplicate rolls
must be rerolled. Iron Market Parts Cache can ignore one new repair trait
per Aftermath. Second-hand traits cannot be removed.

Permanent Iron Capacity, maximum Structure Points, SPD and Exhaust changes
are included automatically. A Heat Exchanger trait opens the preceding Heat
space at modifier +0 while retaining the other printed modifiers. Armour,
weapon accuracy/Strength, backfire and Heat-result modifiers are reminders
for manual resolution at the table. Common and faction profiles retain their
printed base combat values.

Buy new or second-hand chassis, equip weapons and Equipment, assign pilots,
claim battlefield rewards, store gear, sell stored items, or scrap chassis.
New chassis cost 4/8/12/16 Salvage and receive 5/5/6/6 Iron for weapons.
Extra weapons and Equipment cost their printed Iron cost in Salvage.
Second-hand chassis cost half and require four trait rolls plus four D3
component damage rolls. The initial Arms trait applies to the left weapon.
Stored weapons retain weapon-specific traits. Compatible stored weapons are
matched to their original chassis profile, preserving different printed
profiles for similarly named weapons.

The Experimental Load-Bearing Frame can be assigned to another owned Rig.
A lost target must be reassigned before the affected Rig can deploy.
Mercenary hiring is once per campaign, for one Engagement. Mercenaries use
a separate support slot and their contractor allowance, cannot secure
objectives or earn XP, and leave after the Engagement. Their gear cannot be
transferred to the player's inventory. Agree their pilot grade at the table.

REVIEWABLE GROUP RULINGS AND MANUAL ALLOCATIONS
The manual leaves some details open; the app shows the choice instead of
silently treating an interpretation as an additional printed rule:
- Environmental damage: choose affected components at the table. If there
  are fewer eligible Rigs, only the available targets are required.
- Free repair credits: the checkbox treats one credit as one selected repair
  operation; use that only with group agreement, or record an adjustment.
- Battlefield Recovery Rig: add the agreed D3 number of extra search rolls
  separately from the normal per-Rig search.
- Settlement: choose whether reserve/unassigned pilots also receive its XP.
- Underdog: choose the applicable D3 lower-value or D6 short-handed award.
  Compare the post-reward roster values with the opponent; scenario
  adjustments can record a different interpretation or mission rule.
- Free battlefield chassis are treated as new recruits with a normal weapon
  allowance. Adjust if your group uses a different interpretation.
- Salvage result 13: only a Rig destroyed in that Engagement is eligible.
  Enter its actual component state. No weapon allowance is granted. Use the
  recorded adjustment tools to reconstruct inherited gear and traits from
  its original sheet. If no eligible Rig exists, remove the unused wreck
  claim with a reasoned adjustment.

The app does not simulate all combat, arbitrate mission objectives or keep
an opponent's hidden roster. Those facts are supplied in the report.
Scenario adjustments, inherited wreck details and agreed rulings are logged.
The report handles reward accounting and progression together; you make
repair purchases in the hangar after reviewing/applying the report. Keep
any order-sensitive scenario exceptions aligned with the group's rules.
